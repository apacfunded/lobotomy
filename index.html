Setup · fee configs

Creates one on-chain fee config per insurance level. Every coin launched on your site uses one of them, which is what routes your fee to you. You only do this once (it costs about 0.02 SOL per config).

Defaults match pump.fun's curve: coins start around 23.5 SOL market cap and graduate to a Meteora pool at about 406 SOL. Creation fee: 0 = free like pump.fun (if set, it must be at least 0.001 SOL).
Anti-snipe configs let creators launch with a 50% trading fee that falls to the normal fee over the first 60 seconds. Snipers and bundle bots pay most of it; the creator's own dev buy at launch pays the normal fee. 6 more approvals, about 0.04 SOL.

          
        

      


      

        
Register launchpad

        
Tags your fee configs on-chain with your launchpad's name, website and logo. This is what you point trading sites (Jupiter, Axiom, GMGN, Dexscreener…) to when you ask them to show your badge on your coins. One time, costs a fraction of a cent.

        

          

          

          

        

        
        

      


      

        
Fees & insurance

        

        
Distribute now pays every holder who is down right now in one click, with no 24-hour wait. Nobody is paid past break-even, and earlier payouts are always subtracted.

        

        
Fees are collected while a coin is on the bonding curve. After graduation, your locked LP position in the Meteora pool keeps earning fees; collect those from your Meteora portfolio (app.meteora.ag) and pay the insurance share out here with a manual budget.

      


      

        
Buy back & burn

        
Spends SOL from your wallet to buy your main coin on Jupiter, then burns exactly what was bought. Burned tokens are gone forever, which shrinks supply. Each fee collection adds the buyback share (0.25% of every trade, set by SITE.BUYBACK_PCT) to the amount below. The bot does this automatically every hour.
